# Competitor Radar Archetypes & Benchmark Reference

This reference defines industry-specific metrics and peer group selection rules.

---

## 1. Industry Archetype Metric Sets

| Archetype | Primary Moat Metric | Secondary Efficiency Metric | Capital Structure Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fabless Semiconductors** | Gross Margin (IP pricing power) | Operating Margin / R&D conversion | Net Cash / Market Cap (cushion for foundry prepayments) |
| **Enterprise Software / SaaS** | Net Retention Rate (NRR) | Operating Margin / FCF Conversion | EV / Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) |
| **Consumer Retail / Platforms** | Take Rate & Logistics Density | Inventory Turnover Ratio | Free Cash Flow Conversion |
| **Industrial / Capital Goods** | Aftermarket Parts Margin Mix | Return on Invested Capital (ROIC) | Net Debt / EBITDA (covenant safety) |
| **Commercial Banking** | Net Interest Margin (NIM) | Efficiency Ratio (<55%) | CET1 Ratio & Tangible Common Equity (TCE) |

---

## 2. Peer Group Tiering Hierarchy

1. **Target Anchor:** The company currently being analyzed.
2. **Direct Product Competitor:** Companies competing head-to-head for identical customer contracts and sockets.
3. **High-Multiple Benchmark:** High-quality, diversified market leaders (TXN, ADI, MSFT, etc.) used to gauge the theoretical ceiling multiple if the target diversifies its customer concentration.
4. **Low-Multiple / Distressed Benchmark:** Lower-tier or commoditized peers used to determine the valuation floor if the target loses its technological edge.
